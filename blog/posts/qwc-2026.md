I’m a PhD candidate at Kent State. My research is on quantum sensors and medical robotics. From Sept 23 to 25, 2026, I was at Quantum World Congress, held at The Hotel at the University of Maryland in College Park, Maryland.

![Group photo at the Quantum World Congress 2026 photo wall in College Park, Maryland](../assets/blog/qwc-2026.webp "Quantum World Congress 2026, College Park, Maryland")

I went as part of the NSF I-Corps program. The work was customer discovery, so the days looked like meetings more than lectures. I met industry leaders and entrepreneurs in quantum and asked what problems they face. It is a slower conversation than a lab meeting. Someone tells you what is hard in their own work, and you listen instead of jumping to a fix. I tried to keep each conversation on their problem, not on a pitch.

Quantum sensors are why I wanted to be in those rooms. I study them at Kent State, and medical robotics sits beside that work. I wanted to hear how people outside the university talk about sensing. I came home with a better sense of the questions worth carrying back to the lab.

The public page for the congress is [quantumworldcongress.com](https://www.quantumworldcongress.com/). The photo above is from the photo wall in College Park.
