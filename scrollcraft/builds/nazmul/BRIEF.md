## Grammar
Chaptered editorial.

The other seven lost:
- Filmic one-shot forbids jumping. A portfolio has to open publications, the CV, and contact directly.
- Live surface is for a product you operate. This page is a person.
- Continuous world needs one geography and an unbroken flight. The story cuts between lab, hospitals, and software.
- Typographic poster would hide the real portrait and the project screenshots.
- Gallery/catalog makes every section an object label. The visitor's first question is who he is.
- Split stage needs two columns in tension for the whole page. There is no pair of sides to resolve.
- Rhythmic cutlist is a pulse. This material wants to be read.
