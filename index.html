About

I started with MRI and X-ray at Siemens Bangladesh and taught part-time at Daffodil, then spent eight years on CT and PET CT scanners at Philips and three keeping Medtronic's Hugo surgical robots running.

Now I build assistive and wearable devices for rehabilitation. I've published on AI that explains how it reads CT scans, including kidney findings and lung disease.
